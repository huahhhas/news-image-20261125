One image summing up the world's news of **Wednesday 25 November 2026**:
six stories from the day, across regions and fields.

1. **USA · Cyberattack** — CIA and US intelligence hit by a cyber breach; unclassified data exposed.
2. **Europe · War at sea** — Ukraine's drone strikes on Russian ships draw threats; NATO jets head to Poland.
3. **Middle East** — Drone hits Riyadh's tallest tower; 11 people hurt, Houthis blamed.
4. **Africa · Health** — WHO pulls Ebola health workers from a Congo hospital after days sheltering under fire.
5. **Science** — Euclid spots the oldest known quasar, a black hole shining 630 million years after the Big Bang.
6. **Economy** — Iran's rial falls to a record low after US sanctions on Tehran's "shadow banking" network.

Sources: Reuters, CNN, AP, ESA — reported 25 November 2026.

## How it works

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib, no network) copies `entry.png` — the
1024×1024 image drawn by `draw_news.py` — to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output/entry.png`).
Two runs write byte-identical output.
